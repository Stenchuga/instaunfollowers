About us

Beekeeping with tradition

Stanojević Beekeeping is a family apiary from Smederevska Palanka, run by Goran Stanojević – president of the “Jasenica” Beekeepers' Society and an electrical engineering teacher. Besides honey and other bee products, we collect bee venom, build our own Apitox venom collectors – awarded at the national beekeeping fair – make cosmetics based on bee venom and propolis, and run an api-chamber where visitors breathe the air from the hive.

Welcome!

Bee products – honey, pollen, royal jelly and propolis – are at the very top of natural, healthy food. Bees have given us an ideal food that meets all the needs of the human body.
