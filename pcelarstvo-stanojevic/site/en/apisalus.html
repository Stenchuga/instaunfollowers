ApiSalus

ApiSalus – bee health – thanks to capsaicin, one of the cream's ingredients, apitoxin (bee venom) quickly penetrates the skin and improves circulation and blood flow in the peripheral parts of the body. The most important components of apitoxin are melittin and adolapin. Melittin plays a role in lowering blood pressure, prevents blood clotting, has an antibacterial effect, protects against radiation and acts on the central nervous system. Adolapin helps relieve pain and has an anti-rheumatic effect.

Ingredients
Bee venom, virgin olive oil, virgin hemp oil, beeswax, manuka honey, distilled water, eucalyptus essential oil, rosemary essential oil, chili pepper extract and vitamin E.
How to use
Apply and rub into the target area. Repeat 2–3 times a day.
Note
People allergic to bee venom must not use the cream without consulting a doctor.
