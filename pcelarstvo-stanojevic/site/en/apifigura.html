ApiFigura

ApiFigura – anti-wrinkle – the only cream with bee venom on our market that also contains freeze-dried royal jelly. Besides tightening your wrinkles, it will leave your face soft and nourished. Warmly recommended for people with problem skin, such as acne.

Ingredients
Bee venom, virgin argan oil, virgin olive oil, castor oil, shea butter, cocoa butter, geranium essential oil, beeswax, manuka honey, distilled water, vitamin E.
How to use
Apply and rub into the target area (1–2 times a day).
Note
People allergic to bee venom must not use the cream without consulting a doctor. After opening, the cream remains stable for 6 months. Store at temperatures up to 35 °C.
